      Attempts this session

        Default targets: 100–120 compressions per minute; each compression reaches the bottom band (2.2–2.4 in) and returns to the top band (within 0.2 in of the top). Adult CPR guidance is 2.0–2.4 in (5–6 cm) with full chest recoil. Confirm thresholds against current AHA guidelines before using results for certification.

        Settings

        A compression is correct when it reaches the bottom band and the chest comes back up into the top band before the next one. It is incomplete when it misses either band on the way down or on the way up.
